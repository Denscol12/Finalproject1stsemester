What is Sim Racing?

It's a realistic form of virtual motorsport that uses advanced physics software and specialized hardware to mimic real-world car handling, tire grip, and laser-scanned tracks.

Sim racing is often used to train drivers for the actual races to get better outcomes and improve themselves.

What equipment is used?

The equipment depends on the budget and on the kind os sim racing you're doing (e.g. drifting, rallying or circuit racing) but it often comes with steering wheel, pedals, shifter (manual or sequeantial), and a handbrake. The equipment mimics the real world interior parts of the car.

What games do they use for Sim-Racing

There are many games for sim racing but it often depends on what kind of racing, for example IRacing is best for circuit racing
